Story

How Oyen was born.

Four friends, one shared obsession with games and internet cats, and a game jam that turned a joke into a studio.

Chapter 1: The beginning

Four friends, one obsession

Oyen Gameworks started as four friends in Indonesia who loved two things: making games and laughing at cat memes. We wanted to build something small, funny, and genuinely fun to collect, the way mystery card packs felt as kids.

Chapter 2: GAMESEED 2026 Mobile

A jam entry called Catty Cardy

We joined the GAMESEED 2026 Mobile game jam and built Catty Cardy: a casual card-collecting game where every card is inspired by viral cat stickers. Absurd, hilarious, and unmistakably ours.

Chapter 3: From jam to prototype

Polish, release, repeat

After the jam we kept polishing: smoother pack openings, juicier reveals, and update v1.0.1, released free for Android (66 MB) on itch.io. No generative AI, just hand-driven art and code.

Chapter 4: Today

A tiny studio with big meow energy

Today Oyen Gameworks is a four-person squad, the Oyenians, shipping small games, listening to players, and cooking up the next pack of chaos.
